about=QGIS Doctor helps you understand why your project isn't working as expected —
 even when you're not sure what went wrong or where to look.

 When something feels off — layers misaligned, tools behaving strangely,
 settings that don't seem to do anything — the cause is rarely obvious.
 It could be a configuration mistake you made without realizing it, a conflict
 between settings, a broken data source, or a known bug in QGIS itself.

 QGIS Doctor inspects your live QGIS environment — not just the project file,
 but the actual state of the program as it's running — and gives you a plain-language
 report of what it finds, sorted by severity and type.

 For each issue, it tells you whether the problem is something you can fix yourself
 right now, a known bug already reported to the QGIS developers, or something that
 may not have been seen before.

 Optionally, connect your preferred AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or any
 OpenAI-compatible model) via API key. The full context of your QGIS environment
 is sent to the AI, which can then walk you through a solution step by step,
 tailored to your exact situation.

 No AI key? Use the Copy for AI button to paste your report into any chatbot manually.
